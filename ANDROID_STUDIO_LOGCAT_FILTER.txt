==========================================
ANDROID STUDIO LOGCAT FILTER FOR BLACK SCREEN
==========================================

Copy and paste this into Android Studio's Logcat filter bar:

(tag:LoginActivity | level:ERROR | message:inflate | message:Exception | message:NullPointer) & package:mine

==========================================
ALTERNATIVE SIMPLE FILTERS:
==========================================

Filter 1 (Errors + LoginActivity):
(tag:LoginActivity | level:ERROR) & package:mine

Filter 2 (Comprehensive):
tag:LoginActivity | tag:AndroidRuntime | level:ERROR | tag:ActivityTaskManager | message:Exception

Filter 3 (Layout Issues):
(tag:LoginActivity | message:inflate | message:ResourceNotFound | tag:LayoutInflater) & package:mine

Filter 4 (Activity Lifecycle):
(tag:LoginActivity | tag:ActivityTaskManager | message:ActivityRecord | level:ERROR) & package:mine

==========================================
QUICK REFERENCE:
==========================================

Use these operators:
- | (pipe) = OR
- & (ampersand) = AND  
- tag: = Filter by log tag
- level: = Filter by log level (ERROR, WARN, INFO, DEBUG, VERBOSE)
- message: = Filter by message content
- package:mine = Filter by your app's package
